What changed from v1 — read this first
The target moved from HeroUI Native to HeroUI v3 for React, so DESIGN.md §3–§4 no longer describe the available set. Three things v1 had to hand-build now ship in the library: Table, TimeField and DatePicker. Every annotation below uses real v3 export names and the compound Card.Header / ListBox.Item dot notation.
Brand now comes from your screens
Your mockups are green, so green it is — the original “avoid green” note in the brief is overridden by your own designs. Name is MasjidLove. Deep green is the accent, your dark teal maps to HeroUI’s variant="secondary", and the blue from your “Forgot Password?” link becomes --link. Because the accent is green, there is no separate success colour — a green “confirmed” chip next to a green accent carries no information. Confirmed states use --accent-soft.
Emphasis moved to events
Jamaat times are now a single quiet line at the top of Home with a link to the full week; the screen’s weight sits on event cards with real imagery. The 96px countdown from v1 is gone. Times remain fully available — one tap away, and still the whole of screens 15 and 19 — they just stopped being the thing you see first.
Onboarding — 11 screens
Your four screens re-imagined in HeroUI v3, plus four that the flow needs to actually work: email verification, masjid picking, notification choice, and a close. No carousel — every screen either collects something or grants something.
